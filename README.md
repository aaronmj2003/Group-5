**Problem Statement:** Lack of a Unified Platform for Campus Engagement at UW-Seattle

_Gap:_ Students at UW lack a centralized, accessible platform to connect with peers, discover events, and stay informed about campus activities. Current
methods for sharing information, such as social media or scattered university pages, are fragmented, making it difficult to find a comprehensive view of "what's
going on" around campus.

_Orientation:_ This problem became evident from feedback highlighting how students miss opportunities due to poor visibility of events, gatherings, and other
campus happenings. The trend reflects a growing demand for digital solutions that simplify engagement and foster community connections in a large university
setting.

_Impact:_ The absence of a unified platform reduces student engagement, isolates individuals, and wastes time as students search for relevant activities across
disparate sources.

_Importance:_ A centralized online platform would empower students to connect, stay informed, and engage fully in the UW community, fostering a vibrant
campus culture.

**MVP Description:**
Through a user-friendly interface, students can browse event listings, view promotional media, and interact with event organizers and attendees..Key features include UW NetID authentication, QR code-based event linking, and a discussion forum to facilitate engagement. Unlike Huskylink, Bulletin Board focuses on event-specific interactions rather than Registered Student Organizations (RSOs), ensuring a streamlined experience tailored to student interests.
